BRAND BRIEF:
{{BRAND_BRIEF_JSON}}

ARTICLE CONTENT:
{{ARTICLE_CONTENT}}

TASK:
Analyze the article and produce a Twitter cover image design specification as JSON.

RULES:
1. detected_language: Detect from article content. Use "zh" if the title or content is primarily Chinese characters. Use "en" if primarily English. If language_override is provided (not "none"), use that value.

2. color_scheme: MUST match detected_language. "dark" for "en", "light" for "zh". This is the rule: English articles get dark backgrounds, Chinese articles get light backgrounds.

3. cover_title: A compelling, concise title for the cover. NOT necessarily the same as the article title — improve it for visual impact. Max 60 English characters or 20 Chinese characters.

4. image_prompt: A detailed, vivid prompt for Recraft V4 image generation. MUST:
   - Be at least 80 words
   - Describe ONLY visual elements: subject, lighting, mood, composition, colors, style
   - Include: one strong central visual metaphor relevant to the article topic
   - Include: cinematic lighting description
   - Include: "strong negative space in the lower portion of the image, suitable for text overlay"
   - MUST NOT contain: text, typography, letters, words, logos, brand names, watermarks
   - For dark (English) covers: deep dark background (#0A0A0A), neon accent tones, high contrast
   - For light (Chinese) covers: clean bright background, muted professional tones, editorial feel

5. negative_prompt: List elements to avoid. Always include: "text, words, letters, typography, watermark, logo, cluttered background, multiple competing subjects, generic stock photo aesthetic"

6. layout_spec.template_variant: Choose based on content type:
   - A: General editorial, thought leadership, trend analysis
   - B: Data-heavy, research, statistics, technical deep dives
   - C: Opinion, commentary, hot takes, debate topics

7. layout_spec.overlay_opacity: How much to darken/lighten the image for text readability. Use 0.3 for subtle, 0.5 for medium, 0.65 for strong. Dark covers use dark overlay, light covers use light overlay.

8. brand_checklist: List 3–5 specific brand rules this design follows from the brand brief.
